## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the operational rigor, financial compliance, and organizational clarity required by enterprise HR and payroll operations. Tailored for enterprise administrators, HR directors, financial controllers, and global employees, the visual language balances authoritative gravitas with effortless utilitarian speed.

The overarching design aesthetic is **Corporate / Minimal with High-Information Density**. It relies on crisp typographic hierarchy, precise hairline borders, restrained elevation, and deliberate, desaturated semantic states. The interface discards decorative flourishes in favor of geometric clarity, strict visual order, and uninterrupted tabular alignment, evoking trust, reliability, and institutional precision.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive atmosphere with deep slate navies, precise cool grays, and functional semantic accents.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**: Light mode relies on pristine `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and elevated containers, backed by `#F8FAFC` as the canvas ground. Dark mode reverses this hierarchy with deep slate black `#0B0F17` as the floor canvas and `#111827` / `#1E293B` for layered structural surfaces.
- **Brand & Structural Slate**: `#0F172A` delivers authoritative contrast for headers, primary action buttons, and dominant chrome. `#1E293B` operates as an interactive hover and secondary structural tier.
- **Action & Interactive Accents**: Precision Royal Blue (`#2563EB` light, `#3B82F6` dark) focuses user focus onto interactive links, selected tab lines, and primary focus rings.
